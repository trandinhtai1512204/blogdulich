# City Line Art Assets

Put city hero line-art banners in this folder.

Naming:
- Use city slug names: `ha-noi.png`, `da-nang.png`, `hoi-an.png`.
- Prefer wide PNG/WebP exports, around 21:9.
- Keep the artwork on a white or transparent background.
- Avoid screenshots that include the generator UI, browser chrome, cards, or rounded preview frames.

Examples:
- `/city-line-art/ha-noi.png`
- `/city-line-art/da-nang.webp`
